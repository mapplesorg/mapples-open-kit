## 2.0.0

### Major Changes

- cffb0d6: Mapples 2.0: every `@mapples/*` and `@mapplesorg/*` package is released as 2.0.0, together with the `mapples` CLI 2.0.0. Apps generated by the CLI depend on `@mapples/*` `^2.0.0`.

### Minor Changes

- 83ee37f: `FONT_WEIGHTS` gains `ExtraBold` and `Black`; families list the weights they ship, and `STANDARD_FONT_WEIGHTS` holds the usual four. Archivo now ships `Archivo-ExtraBold` and `Archivo-Black` (a fashion display face), and Fraunces gains `Fraunces-Medium`. A family's weights in `FONT_META` are the only names registered for it, so pick a weight from `FONT_META[family].weights`.
- f16f153: Lexend now ships `Lexend-ExtraBold` (800), so wide display numbers and headlines can use it; `FONT_META.Lexend.weights` lists it.
- bb31d1d: Nine more bundled families for the app templates — Inter Tight, Nunito Sans, Jost, Lexend, Archivo, Bricolage Grotesque, Syne, Instrument Serif (Regular only) and Cormorant Garamond — 21 in all, registered per weight like the rest.
- a6c5872: All `@mapples/*` runtime packages now share one version and are versioned and published together from `mapplesorg/mapples-kit`; `mapples-open-kit` is a read-only mirror of the source.

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 40751c7: Bundled font library: `@mapples/fonts` ships 12 OFL families (DM Sans, Fraunces, IBM Plex Mono, Inter, Manrope, Nunito, Outfit, Playfair Display, Plus Jakarta Sans, Poppins, Sora, Space Grotesk) registered per weight, and `MapplesApp`/`MapplesAppProvider` load `FONT_FILES` alongside the project's own fonts.
