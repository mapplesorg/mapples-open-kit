Native canvases can bridge contexts across a portal boundary: `@mapples/dnd` exports `FrameSpaceContext` and its root pan gesture takes a single pointer so two-finger gestures reach the content; `@mapples/form` exports `FormContext`; `@mapples/state` exports `StateContext`.
