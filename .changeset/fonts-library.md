Bundled font library: `@mapples/fonts` ships 12 OFL families (DM Sans, Fraunces, IBM Plex Mono, Inter, Manrope, Nunito, Outfit, Playfair Display, Plus Jakarta Sans, Poppins, Sora, Space Grotesk) registered per weight, and `MapplesApp`/`MapplesAppProvider` load `FONT_FILES` alongside the project's own fonts.
